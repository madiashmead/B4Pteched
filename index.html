What Does AI Do?

Artificial intelligence is software that learns patterns from data and uses them to make predictions, decisions, and new content.

The short version

Traditional programs follow rules a person writes by hand. AI systems are different: they are trained on large amounts of examples and figure out the rules themselves. Once trained, they can handle new situations they have never seen before.

How it works, in three steps

  1. Collect data Photos, text, sounds, numbers — lots of examples of the task.
  2. Train a model The model adjusts millions of internal settings until its guesses match the examples.
  3. Make predictions The trained model is given something new and produces an answer.

What AI can't do (yet)

  • It doesn't truly understand things the way people do.
  • It can be confidently wrong, so its answers should be checked.
  • It can repeat biases found in the data it learned from.
  • It has no feelings, goals, or opinions of its own.
